Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was a renowned Indian scientist and the 11th President of India. He was born in Tamil Nadu and is widely known for his contributions to the field of aerospace engineering.

Early Life

Kalam was born into a poor family in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. His father was a boat owner and his mother was a housewife.

Important Achievement

Kalam played a crucial role in the development of India's missile technology and is often referred to as the "Missile Man of India." His work on the Agni and Prithvi missiles has been instrumental in enhancing India's defense capabilities.

Words That Inspire

One of Kalam's most famous quotes is: Don't take rest after your success, but keep moving towards your next goal.
